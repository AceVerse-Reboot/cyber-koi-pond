**Cyber Koi Pond** was created by **Dr Asegul Hulus (Ace)** as an AI and cybersecurity themed visual extension of **git-koi**, created by **Anthony Yalong (@yalongwastaken)**.

- Original project: https://github.com/yalongwastaken/git-koi
- git-koi generates the koi from public GitHub activity. Its collection logic, fish anatomy, growth, colours and original swimming frames are upstream work.
- Cyber Koi Pond adds the pond scenery, robotic overlay, oval swimming path, AI emblem, deep-learning motif, ML chip, cybersecurity shield, data signals and shoreline wavelets.
- Parts of the workflow were adapted from git-koi's profile-workflow example. Its original MIT notice is preserved in `LICENSE.git-koi`.

This is an independent extension, not an official git-koi release or an endorsement by its author. The AI and security graphics are decorative; no ML model is trained or executed, and the project does not monitor security threats.
